# Mission and values

Blossom Labs is an open source project building a desktop platform for digital independence: powerful enough for developers, approachable enough for anyone, and built to meet the sovereignty and privacy standards European individuals and institutions need.

- Modern operating systems have drifted from users: forced updates, dark patterns, built-in data collection, unwanted software, and for European users and institutions, personal data flowing to foreign servers with no regard for sovereignty.
- Pixel-perfect by default: if something in the UI/UX is wrong, Blossom Labs fixes it directly instead of waiting for someone upstream to.
- Less fragmentation: BlossomUI and Arc exist to make the Linux desktop and its software feel like one coherent system instead of a patchwork of formats and styles.
- Privacy first: no unwanted telemetry, no data sold, no foreign servers, GDPR-compliant from the ground up.
- Open source: every line of code is public.
- Community driven: development happens in the open with contributors and users.
- Built to last: stability and longevity, no foreign vendor lock-in.
